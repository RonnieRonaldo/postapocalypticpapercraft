01 / THE WORLD

A World Built from Paper

Welcome to a world of forgotten roads, abandoned buildings and industrial decay. At Post-Apocalyptic Papercraft, we turn the atmosphere of deserted places and urban ruins into printable DIY diorama projects you can build at home. Explore miniature gas stations, weathered architecture, rusty machinery and handcrafted display scenery inspired by a world after the apocalypse. Our paper and cardboard creations are for hobbyists who enjoy building physical miniature worlds without needing a 3D printer. From a single abandoned roadside scene to a compact shelf display, every project tells its own wasteland story.

Explore the Gas Station Box, a printable abandoned roadside diorama →

02 / THE SCALE

Small Displays. Big Wasteland Stories.

A memorable miniature display doesn’t need an entire gaming table. Sometimes all it takes is a damaged wall, a weathered floor, a forgotten workshop or a tiny corner of a ruined city. Our growing collection focuses on compact display dioramas, miniature room scenes and printable backdrops for collectors, creative makers and tabletop enthusiasts. Build a DIY box diorama for a favorite miniature figure, create atmospheric scenery for toy photography or add a handcrafted wasteland display to your shelf. As the collection grows, we plan to introduce modular floors, miniature props and industrial backdrops to expand your post-apocalyptic scenes.

03 / THE IDEA

Print. Build. Survive.

Rusty gas stations. Abandoned workshops. Gothic oddities. Forgotten machines. Discover a growing world of printable papercraft, miniature scenery and unusual apocalyptic decor for gamers, collectors and DIY enthusiasts. Whether you’re looking for gothic gamer decor, a small display diorama, a backdrop for tabletop roleplay or a weathered miniature scene for photography, the idea is simple: create atmospheric worlds from ordinary paper and cardboard. No expensive equipment or 3D printer required. Print, cut, fold, glue and bring your little wasteland to life.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

What is post-apocalyptic papercraft?

Printable paper and cardstock designs for miniature wasteland scenes, abandoned buildings and unusual decorative objects.

Do I need a 3D printer?

No. Our planned papercraft projects use paper or cardstock, scissors, glue and basic craft materials.

Are the displays suitable for 28–32mm miniatures?

Selected upcoming displays are planned for 28–32mm figures. Check the dimensions on each individual product page.

Will A4 and US Letter versions be included?

We intend to offer both formats where practical. Each product page will specify which PDF files are included.

Can I buy these models now?

The Gas Station Box has its own product preview page. Checkout and secure PDF downloads will become available after final testing. The other collections are still in development.
