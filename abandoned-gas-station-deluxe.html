Abandoned Places and Urban Ruins, Recreated in Miniature

Imagine a forgotten service station along an empty highway, with rusted fuel pumps, worn signs and discarded supplies. The Deluxe paper model recreates the atmosphere of abandoned places, industrial decay and a post-apocalyptic roadside scene in a compact display. The printed cardboard backdrop gives the finished diorama depth without requiring an entire ruined city or a large tabletop gaming board.

For diecast collectors, this miniature display scenery creates a setting for 1:43 scale model cars. It can also work as apocalyptic terrain, wasteland decor or a toy photography backdrop. Compare your vehicle dimensions with the finished display before building, as model-car sizes vary.

DIY Building, Print at Home

Download the A4 or US Letter model sheets and print at 100% / Actual Size. Cut, fold and glue the paper pieces, reinforce the base and backdrop with cardboard, and arrange the included miniature props to tell your own abandoned roadside story. This is a printable papercraft DIY building, not a 3D-printer file or a physical kit shipped by mail.

The combination of a ruined building, scrap tires, barrel and weathered scenery makes a satisfying collector gift project for someone who enjoys miniature worlds and handmade diecast displays.
